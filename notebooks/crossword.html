The LA Times crosswords
I like their Midi and Daily crosswords. A bit more difficult than Seattle Times' but also I find them more clever and often wittier.
Chris Remo's YouTube page
Daily videos where Chris Remo solves all NY Times puzzles. Impressive skill.
