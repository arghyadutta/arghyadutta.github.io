Crossword Puzzle

Recommended

The Seattle Times
The Seattle Times' game page lists The NY Times Mini and full crosswords along with their own Midi, Modern Midi, and Large crosswords, which are quite decent.
The LA Times
I like their Midi and Daily crosswords. A bit more difficult than Seattle Times' but I find them cleverer and wittier. I think the difficult increases from Monday through Saturday, like the NYTimes.
Chris Remo's YouTube page
Daily videos where Chris Remo solves all NY Times puzzles. Impressive.
